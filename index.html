✈️ Part 3: Kingston Post-Match Meal & Charter Flight Strategy (Sat Nov 14 → Sun Nov 15)

AC Hotel Kingston → ~1:00 AM Cincinnati Arrival

⚽ Making Kingston Post-Match Healthy, Fun & Recovery-Focused

  • What Works Well Now: Chimichurri Steak Tips (iron/zinc), Lemon Pepper Salmon (omega-3s), and the Açaí Station (polyphenols) provide strong recovery foundations.
  • Discussion Ideas for the Chef:
    • Consider an authentic Jamaican touch (e.g., grilled jerk chicken/snapper or festival bread) so the away meal feels memorable.
    • Discuss lighter alternatives for the heavy Mac N Cheese / Roasted Brussels Sprouts post-match sides.
    • At the Açaí & Ice Cream station, pair with recovery boosters (tart cherry, whey/plant protein, mango/pineapple, chia).

🛫 Charter Flight Snacks (Kingston → Cincinnati)

  • Cabin Hydration: Tart cherry shots + high-electrolyte fluids at boarding to counter cabin altitude dehydration and support sleep onset.
  • Slow-Release Protein Before Sleep: Greek yogurt parfaits, cottage cheese bowls, or a light turkey/avocado wrap for late-digestion amino acid delivery.
  • Clean Grab-and-Go Basket: 100% natural single-squeeze nut butter packs, bananas, clementines, dates, dark chocolate almonds, and low-sugar jerky/biltong.
