# USMNT November 2026 Camp — Menu Planning Board & Master Recipe Book

Interactive card-based drag-and-drop menu planning board, 258-item Hotel BEO Master Recipe Book, 20-Point Dietitian & Culinary Discussion Guide, and 19 USMNT Menu Audit Rules (`Rules 1.1–6.2`).

## Features
- **Menu Planning Board (Drag & Drop):** Columns represent the 12 camp days (`Nov 7–18, 2026` across Atlanta, Kingston, and Cincinnati) and rows represent Meal & Category swimlanes (`Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Dinner / Pre-Match`, `Post-Match`). Drag and drop recipe cards between days, swap items, or drag from the Recipe & Swap Library.
- **Master Recipe Book (258 Recipes):** Searchable Hotel BEO recipes with dynamic batch scaling (`1x / 10 portions`, `2x / 20 portions`, `3x / 30 portions`).
- **Dietitian & Culinary Audit:** 20-point collaborative review guide with one-click highlights into the planning board.
- **USMNT Menu Audit Rules (`1.1–6.2`):** Complete 19-rule evaluation matrix.
- **Client-Side Excel (`.xlsx`) Export:** Download the updated menu plan in the USMNT Excel layout directly from the browser.
